# Quiet Luxury Fashion: B2B Textile Manufacturing Engineering & EU EPR Lab

An interactive textile engineering simulator and B2B technical whitepaper developed by **[Conbello Textile](https://www.conbello.com)** (ConTex Group, Istanbul, Turkey). 

This tool demonstrates the measurable engineering differences between **Quiet Luxury apparel manufacturing** and **fast-fashion minimalism** across everyday essentials and performance activewear (T-shirts, heavyweight hoodies, women's sculpt/gym leggings, and technical sports bras).

## Key Engineering Modules in This Interactive Lab

1. **Consumer Shift & Interactive CAD Specification:** Visualizes why modern luxury and fitness apparel brands replace loud logos with long-staple compact yarns, high-tenacity polyamides, architectural drape, and tone-on-tone micro-details.
2. **Wash Cycle & Degradation Simulator (0–50 Washes):** Simulates physical stress testing, comparing dimensional stability, shrinkage, seam spirality (torque), collar collapse ("bacon neck"), surface pilling (ISO 12945-2), and plastisol dye migration.
3. **3 Core Engineering Pillars & Forensic Defect Analysis:** Breaks down raw material selection, ergonomic pattern drafting, and the 4 structural failures of resin-bonded regenerated scrap fabrics.
4. **EU Eco-Modulation (EPR) Liability Calculator:** Estimates financial penalties (Malus) vs. durability fee reductions (Bonus) under European Union textile regulations, highlighting how **Mill Generic Test Reports & Technical Data Sheets (TDS)** protect small-batch orders (300–1,000 units).
